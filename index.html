      Tips for better results

      Keep it sharp

      Blurry photos are the main cause of wrong letters. Hold the camera steady and use good light.

      Keep it straight

      Straighten the page and crop out the background before you scan.

      Pick the language

      Choosing the right language improves accuracy a lot.

      Check the result

      OCR is never perfect. Review names, numbers, and symbols before you use the text.

      Privacy Policy

      Your images and PDFs are read in your browser and are never uploaded to our server. The first time you extract text, your browser downloads the OCR engine and language data from a public CDN; your file is not sent there. Nothing you process is stored. We count visits anonymously with GoatCounter (pages viewed, country, browser and device type). It uses no cookies and never sees the contents of your files.
